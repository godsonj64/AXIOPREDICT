About

        
AXIO PREDICT is a desktop application for AI-powered lung cancer risk assessment built on the validated Sybil deep-learning model. It processes chest CT scans locally on your hardware — no data leaves your machine.

        
Developed and maintained by Godson Johnson, Axiomatic Research.

Underlying Model — Sybil

        
Sybil is a 3D convolutional neural network (R3D-18 backbone with multi-head attention pooling) trained on the National Lung Screening Trial (NLST) dataset. It predicts the cumulative probability of lung cancer diagnosis within 1–6 years from a single low-dose CT scan, without requiring radiologist annotations.

Validation Datasets

        
    
          
  • NLST — National Lung Screening Trial (USA); 14,851 participants; primary training/validation
    • 
          
  • MGH — Massachusetts General Hospital screening cohort
    • 
          
  • Lahey Hospital — Independent US clinical validation
    • 
          
  • Chang Gung Memorial Hospital (CGMH) — Taiwan; cross-population generalisability
    • 
        


        
Model Architecture

        
    
          
  • Encoder: R3D-18 (3D ResNet-18), pre-trained on Kinetics-400 video dataset
    • 
          
  • Pooling: Multi-head attention (spatial + temporal) + global max pool
    • 
          
  • Output head: Cumulative Probability Layer — upper-triangular masked hazard sum → sigmoid per year
    • 
          
  • Calibration: Per-year isotonic regression (trained on NLST validation set)
    • 
          
  • Ensemble: 5-model ensemble; final score = arithmetic mean of calibrated outputs
    • 
          
  • Input: LDCT resampled to 0.703125 × 0.703125 × 2.5 mm voxel spacing, cropped/padded to 256 × 256 × 200
    • 
        


        
Attention Maps

        
Per-slice attention weights are extracted from image_attention_1 (spatial softmax attention over each CT frame). High-attention regions indicate areas the model focused on when estimating cancer risk. The raw attention map is a (T × H × W) float tensor collapsed to a (H × W) grayscale overlay.


        
Colormap Modes

        
    
          
  • Jet / Hot / Viridis / Plasma / Inferno / Turbo / Cool / Rainbow — Standard scientific colormaps applied as a blended overlay on the CT image
    • 
          
  • Raw Attn — Pure grayscale rendering of the raw model attention weights, without colorization; useful for quantitative inspection
    • 
          
  • CT only — Disables overlay; shows the windowed CT slice (C=−600, W=1500 HU, lung window)
    • 
          
  • Heatmap — Color only, no CT background blend
    • 
          
  • Contour — Edge-only rendering at attention boundaries
    • 
        


        
Probability Calculation Pipeline

        
    
          
  1. CT volume resampled to canonical voxel spacing via TorchIO
    2. 
          
  2. R3D-18 encoder → spatial feature tensor (B × 512 × T × H × W)
    3. 
          
  3. Dual attention pool (spatial + temporal) + global max pool → 3 × 512-dim hidden vectors
    4. 
          
  4. Linear(1536 → 512) → ReLU → Dropout
    5. 
          
  5. Cumulative hazard FC: Linear(512 → T) → ReLU → upper-triangular masked sum + base hazard
    6. 
          
  6. Sigmoid → P(cancer ≤ year t) for t = 1…6
    7. 
          
  7. Ensemble: arithmetic mean over 5 models
    8. 
          
  8. Isotonic regression calibration per year
    9. 
        


        
Regulatory Notice

        

          ⚕ FOR RESEARCH USE ONLY

          AXIO PREDICT and the underlying Sybil model are not FDA-cleared, CE-marked, or validated for clinical diagnostic use. All outputs must be reviewed by a qualified clinical professional before any patient-care decision. HU-based tissue identification is only meaningful for properly calibrated CT acquisitions with correct RescaleSlope/Intercept.
